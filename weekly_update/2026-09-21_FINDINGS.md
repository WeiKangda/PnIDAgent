# Dataset-P&ID measurements

Dataset: Dataset-P&ID (Paliwal et al., arXiv:2109.03794), 500 synthetic sheets at
7168x4561. Code under evaluation: `PnIDAgent` @ `v31-synth-repro`, on the 8xH100
box (`/mnt/wkd-efs/code/pnid_work`). Session of 2026-09-21.

Caveats that apply to everything below:

- These sheets are clean synthetic drawings (flat grey background, crisp strokes,
  one font). Use them as a development and pretraining set; keep reporting
  headline numbers on the four real held-out drawings ("real4"). real4 has
  symbol GT only; see the real4 section for what could and could not be
  checked there.
- Licensing is unresolved (paper CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, dataset no stated license).
  Nothing derived from it goes into a commercial artifact until cleared. That
  includes the YOLO weights and the line segmenter trained here.
- **Sheet sets.** Drive throttling left 83 of 500 sheets with all seven
  annotation arrays by the end of the session (`results/complete_ids.txt`).
  `results/split.json` is a fixed 450/50 split (seed 42) over ids 0..499, used
  by every trained model; `valc_ids.txt` = the 14 complete val-split sheets,
  the only sheets no model has seen. `dev_ids.txt` (56) is the complete set at
  the time the OCR runs started. Every number states its sheet count.

### Landed in `process_text_lines.py`

- `SOLID_CFG`: `target_max_dim` 4096; `min_line_length_at_scale` rescaled with
  it to {168, 130, 102} (capped-image pixels); `notes_keep_ratio` `"auto"`;
  `solidity_filter: False` (`max_transitions` 30 if re-enabled). The notes
  mask is computed once at cap scale and reused per scale.
- **`is_solid_line` is disabled, not replaced.** At `max_transitions=30` it
  rejects almost nothing and what it rejects is more often a true pipe than a
  false one (0.924 with, 0.929 without). The band sampler separates dashed from
  solid cleanly but is stricter on true solids (-3.4 points). Nothing the
  filter does is useful on this dataset; it stays available behind the flag
  because it has not been measured on real4.
- **Notes crop is detected.** `detect_notes_boundary()` looks for the ruled
  notes/title column: horizontal rulings that all start on one vertical line
  and end at the right frame edge, with that vertical line spanning most of the
  frame. 56/56 sheets detected at 0.790 W (min 0.7898, max 0.7903); the
  right-most GT pipe or symbol sits at 0.765 W. Output is byte-identical to the
  fixed 0.78. With no such column nothing is cropped.

### Learned line extractor beats the classical path

`tools/line_seg.py`: U-Net (base 32) over native-resolution 768 px crops,
3 classes (bg / solid / dashed), targets = GT lines rasterised at 3 px. Frame,
tables, text and symbol outlines are negatives, so it learns the notes crop and
symbol suppression itself. Trained on the 45 complete train-split sheets (20
epochs x 400 steps, 25 min on one H100); mask -> segments by HoughLinesP +
collinear merge, with min length 100 px and threshold 60 chosen on the
train sheets. Inference 2.5 s/sheet.

**14 held-out val-complete sheets:**

| line source | solid F1 | all P | R | F1 | buffer F1 | pred/sheet |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| as shipped | 0.803 | 0.886 | 0.753 | 0.812 | 0.851 | 41 |
| classical, landed defaults | 0.878 | 0.992 | 0.886 | 0.936 | 0.947 | 54 |
| learned segmenter | 0.848 | 0.973 | 0.991 | **0.982** | 0.986 | 77 |

Recall is the whole story: 0.886 -> 0.991. The lower solid-only F1 is a metric
artefact (the segmenter also emits dashed segments, which count against the
solid stratum's precision; the classical path labels everything solid). The
segmenter is not yet the stage default: it needs the torch env, and it has
never seen a real drawing. It is wired for A/B through `--pred-json`.

## Task 2 -- symbol detection

All three suspected defects in `finetune_yolo_symbols.py` were real: `nc: 1`
with every label as class 0 and the class column never read; whole sheets at
`imgsz=640` (symbols are 33-155 px, median 69, so ~5 px); annotations expected
under `data_root/<idx>/`. Rewritten: 1280 px tiles with 256 px overlap from the
native sheet, boxes clipped at tile borders and kept when >= 50% visible, 32
real classes, fixed persisted split, mask-png fallback, tiled inference with
cross-tile NMS. 12,609 train tiles (96,891 boxes), 1,750 val tiles.

Two inference defects were found by the sheet-level metric and fixed:
class-aware NMS keeps a second box of another class on some symbols
(`--agnostic_nms`), and a box clipped by a tile border has IoU ~0.5 with the
full box from the neighbouring tile and survives NMS. Dropping boxes that touch
a tile edge took precision at conf 0.25 from 0.877 to 1.000 on 50 sheets.

Training: yolo11m, imgsz 1280, batch 64 on 4 H100s, 60 epochs (~2 min each),
flips and hue off, mild scale/translate. **Sheet-level, `tools/eval_symbols.py`:**

| GT | sheets | boxes | agnostic mAP50 | mAP50-95 | 32-way mAP50 | mAP50-95 | P / R @0.25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `symbols.npy` | 14 val-complete | 1791 | 1.000 | 0.969 | 1.000 | 0.970 | 1.000 / 1.000 |
| mask boxes where npy missing | 50 val | 5998 | 0.990 | 0.977 | 0.999 | 0.986 | 1.000 / 1.000 |

Per class (50 sheets): every class AP50 >= 0.979 (c27 lowest, 167 boxes);
AP50-95 lowest c21 (0.951, the most frequent class, 555 boxes) then c32, c15,
c30, c16. No class is hard on this data; 32-way classification is essentially
solved here, which says more about the synthetic set than about the model.

| D 90-degree, no CLAHE, scale 1.0 | 0.797 | 0.860 | 0.827 | 0.768 | 0.033 | 0.727 | 0.873 | 0.501 | 0.341 |
| G no CLAHE, scale 1.0, border drop (no 90) | 0.922 | 0.856 | 0.887 | 0.823 | 0.027 | 0.787 | 0.903 | 0.772 | 0.482 |
| E = G + 90-degree pass | 0.922 | 0.868 | 0.894 | 0.823 | 0.025 | 0.797 | 0.909 | 0.609 | 0.482 |
| **E + tag-grammar corrector (landed)** | 0.922 | 0.868 | 0.894 | **0.848** | **0.022** | **0.819** | 0.926 | **0.989** | 0.482 |

(`det P` is over all predictions, so it is only meaningful in the `all` row;
per-stratum detection recall is what to read. tag_v is 6 words/sheet.)

What the ladder says:

- **The 1.35x scale is the single biggest loss, not a gain.** F -> D is the
  same run minus the extra scale: detection F1 0.748 -> 0.827, CER halved.
  On a `6"-DW-1865` crop the native-scale reading is right; the 1.35x reading
  is `6-DW-1865` at *higher* confidence, and NMS keeps it. (CLAHE alone was
  not isolated; the probe showed it also drops the inch mark on the rotated
  crop.)
- **Tile-border clipped boxes were winning NMS**: D -> G adds
  `drop_border=4` and is worth +0.06 detection F1 and +0.06 read rate.
- **Vertical tags were not near zero.** The hypothesis was wrong: PaddleOCR
  2.7.3 rotates tall crops internally, so the shipped stage already reads
  vertical tags at exact 0.624 (vs 0.856 horizontal). The 90-degree pass alone
  (B) makes them *worse* (0.376) because its rotated readings drop inch marks
  even more; it only pays after correction: E+corrector 0.989 vs G+corrector
  0.939, read 0.988 vs 0.822. All 363 rotated words on 56 sheets are pipe
  labels `9"-AA-9999`.
- **GT text is 90% nine shapes** (14.5k words): `AA-99999` 25%, `999` 11%,
  `AAA` 9%, `9"-AA-9999` 6%, `AA-99` 4%, `9"`/`99"` 5%, `AAA-9-99` 2%,
  `9"x9"` 2%, `99.` 7%. `PnIDAgent/tag_grammar.py` re-inserts inch marks and
  flips O/0, I/1, S/5, B/8, Z/2, G/6 per slot, accepting a candidate only when
  it turns a non-conforming string into a grammar match; plain words and bare
  digit runs are untouched. Tag CER after correction is 0.019, so recognizer
  fine-tuning on synthetic crops was not pursued: the remaining tag loss is
  detection recall (0.851 on horizontal tags at IoU 0.5), mostly boxes merged
  or split against neighbours.
- Landed as defaults in `step1_paddleocr`: `preprocess="none"`,
  `scales=(1.0,)`, `rotations=(0, 90)`, `drop_border=4`, `tag_correct=True`.
  One scale plus the rotated pass is about the same cost as the shipped two
  scales, minus the denoising.

## Task 4 -- connectivity

Connectivity GT is `dpid.derive_graph()`. Evaluator `tools/eval_graph.py`:
symbol nodes matched by box IoU >= 0.5, junction nodes by nearest position
within 20 px; a predicted edge is correct when both endpoints matched and the
matched pair is a GT edge. Edge P/R/F1, whole-sheet-exact rate, symbol degree
error and degree-exact rate; symbol-closure (`derive_edges`) for comparison.

### The assembler, not detection, was the bottleneck

**GT symbols + GT lines:**

| assembler | sheets | edge P | R | F1 | sheet exact | deg-exact | closure F1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `digitize_pnid` as shipped (endpoint chains) | 56 dev | 0.238 | 0.077 | 0.116 | 0.000 | 0.363 | 0.211 |
| `digitize_pnid` as shipped | 14 val-complete | 0.279 | 0.097 | 0.143 | 0.000 | 0.367 | 0.243 |
| `pnid_graph.build_topology` (new default) | 56 dev | 1.000 | 1.000 | **1.000** | 0.982 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| `pnid_graph.build_topology` | 14 val-complete | 1.000 | 1.000 | **1.000** | 0.929 | 0.999 | -- |

Three causes, measured on sheet 0:

1. **Branches cannot be represented.** 189 of 205 GT pipe runs touch a
   degree-3 or -4 junction; endpoint-to-endpoint chaining splits every T into
   chains that are never linked. Structural, not a tolerance.
2. **The chain reversal never happens.** Both attach-to-front branches at
   `digitize_pnid.py:587-591` do the same `insert(0, j)`; only the comment
   reverses. 80 of 140 consecutive joins are not head-to-tail, so the
   cumulative projection that orders symbols along a chain is scrambled.
3. **Perpendicular-distance association over-attaches, mildly.** 82/90
   symbols associate with exactly their one line; 8 attach to 2-4 because a
   parallel pipe passes within 50 px. Symbols at a pipe's end are handled by
   the endpoint-to-box term, so that part of the hypothesis did not hold.

`PnIDAgent/pnid_graph.py` (default in `digitize_pnid.py`; legacy via
`--assembler chains`): collinear merge; junctions from shared endpoints, T
(endpoint on interior) and X (proper crossing -- GT treats a crossing as a
shared node); symbols attached when a segment enters the box or a dead-end
endpoint stops within `pad` px of it; degree-2 contraction. Junctions are
emitted as nodes of category `junction` in both output JSONs. Two GT-matching
details: a point already shared by two segments does not attach to an adjacent
symbol (a valve just below a T is on the branch), and GT keeps a dead-end node
when a segment runs past a symbol's centre but ends inside its box
(`drop_inbox_ends=False` reproduces that; predictions want `True`).

| symbols | lines | edge P | R | F1 | deg-exact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GT | GT | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.999 |
| GT | classical (landed defaults) | 0.859 | 0.735 | 0.791 | 0.770 |
| GT | learned segmenter | 0.983 | 0.990 | 0.987 | 0.992 |

Read down the columns: the symbol detector costs nothing on this data; the
classical line stage costs 21 points of edge F1 (recall 0.735: missed and
fragmented runs); the learned extractor recovers all but 1.3 of them. Snap
tolerance 8 -> 12 -> 20 gave 0.709 -> 0.749 -> 0.751 on 45 dev-train sheets;
symbol pad 6 vs 12 changed nothing. Whole-sheet-exact with predicted lines is
0 in every row: there is at least one spurious or missed run on every sheet,
so "exact" is not yet a usable product number.

End-to-end on real drawings remains blocked on the private YOLO checkpoint.

## Annotation format gotchas

Repairs `dpid.load_sheet` applies, all verified against the pixels:

1. `symbols` class column mixes `str` and `int` (`'21'` and `21` coexist).
2. `words` bboxes are not consistently `y1<y2` (65/237 inverted on sheet 0).
3. 16/237 `words` rows are zero-area placeholders with empty text.
4. `pipe_label` is present on only 44% of line segments.
5. Folders 245/246/247 hold duplicate uploads (8/11/12 files instead of 7).
6. `linker` is symbol -> (one word, one line), **not** a symbol-to-symbol edge
   list. Connectivity GT has to be derived.
7. ~18% of solid-segment midpoints are not exactly on drawn ink.
8. A third of `linker` rows have no usable tag; symbol-to-tag GT covers ~67%
   of symbols.
9. `lines2` is the sheet frame and title-block rulings, not process pipes.
10. `words` includes title-block and revision-table text (all 20 `KeyValue`
    strings and 26/30 `Table` strings), so OCR scoring has to be stratified.
11. (new) `mask/<idx>_mask.png` is 4562 rows tall against 4561-row images.
12. (new) GT lines run *under* symbols (81/90 symbol centres on sheet 0
    project inside their linked segment, median perpendicular offset 0.5 px);
    Hough output stops at the symbol outline, hence the dead-end attachment
    rule in the assembler.
13. (new) Annotation file names are `_KeyValue.npy` / `_Table.npy` (mixed case).

## Compatibility blockers (still true)

- **OpenCV 5 breaks the line stage** (`HoughLinesP` returns `(N,4)`). Pin
  `opencv-python<5`; paddleocr 2.7.3 itself pins `opencv-python==4.6`.
- The OCR stage targets **PaddleOCR 2.7.x** (`use_gpu`, `use_angle_cls`,
  `ocr.ocr(patch, cls=True)`).
- `paddlepaddle==2.6.2` segfaults on arm64 macOS; `3.0.0`/`3.1.0` work on CPU
  there. Here `paddlepaddle-gpu==3.1.0` (cu126) works on GPU, in its own env.
